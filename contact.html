❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Aditya's Chronicles really 100% free?
Yes! All study materials, PDF notes, interactive sessions, and practice questions are completely free — forever. No hidden charges, no subscriptions.
Are the questions on this platform official NEET/JEE questions?
No. Most questions are generated using Artificial Intelligence (AI) for practice purposes only. They are not official exam questions. Always cross-verify with NCERT textbooks.
Can I download the interactive HTML sessions?
No. Interactive sessions are protected by anti-download measures and can only be viewed/run on the website. PDF notes, however, can be viewed freely.
How can I suggest a new chapter or topic?
Reach out to us on Telegram or Instagram with your suggestion. We love hearing what students want to learn!
I found an error in a question. What should I do?
Please report it on Telegram or Instagram with a screenshot. Since questions are AI-generated, your feedback helps us improve the content quality.
Can I contribute study materials to the platform?
Yes! If you have quality study notes or materials you'd like to share, contact us on Telegram. We'll review and credit you if published.
Do you offer personal mentorship or doubt-solving?
Currently, we don't offer 1-on-1 mentorship. But you can ask doubts in our Telegram channel, and the community will help!
Is my data safe on this website?
Yes. We don't collect personal information. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details.
