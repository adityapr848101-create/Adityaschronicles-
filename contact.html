1 Our Story

Aditya's Chronicles was born out of a simple idea — that quality education should be free and accessible to every student, regardless of their background, location, or financial situation.

As a student preparing for NEET, I realized that many brilliant aspirants struggle not because they lack potential, but because they lack proper resources. Coaching is expensive, and free materials online are often scattered, outdated, or incomplete.

So I decided to build this platform — a one-stop destination where students can find organized, chapter-wise study materials, practice questions, and interactive sessions, all in one place, completely free.

2 Our Mission

🎯 Mission Statement

To empower every NEET, JEE, and CBSE aspirant with free, high-quality, and organized study resources — so that financial constraints never become a barrier to their dreams.

5 About AI-Generated Content

Many of the practice questions and tests on this platform are generated using Artificial Intelligence (AI). We use AI to:

  • Create diverse practice questions quickly
  • Cover a wide range of topics and difficulty levels
  • Provide instant, unlimited practice for students

However, we want to be transparent — AI-generated content may occasionally contain errors or inaccuracies. We strongly recommend students cross-verify all answers with official NCERT textbooks and trusted sources.

For more details, please see our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
